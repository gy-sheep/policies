Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the InOut Stock app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Dreamaru (collectively, the "Application"). Dreamaru is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


Your Records Stay on Your Device

InOut Stock is a fully local application. Every trading record you enter — stock names, purchase and sale prices, quantities, dates, fees, and any calculated profit or loss — is stored only in a database on your own device. The Service Provider operates no servers for this data, has no account system, and has no ability to read, retrieve, or restore your records. This information is never transmitted off your device.

If you use the backup or Excel export features, the Application creates a file and hands it to your device's standard sharing dialog, where you choose what to do with it — save it to a folder, send it to yourself, or pass it to another app. That file contains your trading records, so please treat it with the same care as any other personal document. The Service Provider never receives a copy of it, and where the file goes afterwards is entirely your decision.

Restoring a backup works the same way in reverse: you pick a file from your device, and the Application reads it locally.
