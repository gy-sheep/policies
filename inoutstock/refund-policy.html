Refund Policy

This policy applies to InOut Stock PRO (unlimited stocks, no advertisements, Excel import and export), the in-app purchase offered by the InOut Stock application (the "Application").

1. General Principles

InOut Stock PRO is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Refunds are handled according to the payment policies of the respective store (Google Play, App Store) and, for users in the Republic of Korea, the Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce. Payment and refunds are carried out by the store; the developer does not hold your payment details.

2. How to Request a Refund

Google Play

  • Within 48 hours of purchase: requesting directly from your Google Play order history is the fastest route, in line with Google Play's policy.
  • After 48 hours: as digital content, refunds for a simple change of mind are limited once the service has been made available. Where there is a legitimate reason such as a technical fault or a duplicate charge, please contact the developer including your order number (GPA.0000-0000-0000-00000).

App Store (Apple)

  • Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Requests are handled under Apple's refund policy, and the decision rests with Apple.

3. Limits on Withdrawal of Purchase

Under Article 17(2)5 of the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, the right to withdraw a purchase may be limited once the provision of digital content has begun (for example, once the PRO features have been unlocked). Refunds remain available where the content is defective.

4. Restoring a Purchase

If you change devices or reinstall the Application, you can restore your existing purchase using the "Restore purchases" function inside the Application. You must be signed in with the same store account used for the original purchase.

Restoring a purchase only unlocks the PRO features; it does not restore your trading records. Those are stored on your device alone, so please use the Application's backup feature separately when moving to a new device.

5. Contact

For refund enquiries, contact dreamaru.lab@gmail.com. Requests should be made through the store first; the developer cannot make or overturn a store's refund decision.
